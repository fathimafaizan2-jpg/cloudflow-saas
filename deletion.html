User Data Deletion Instructions

At Cloudflow, we value your privacy and provide a simple way to delete your data from our platform. If you wish to delete your account and all associated automation data, please follow the steps below:

Method 1: Direct In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Log in to your Cloudflow account at cloudflow-app.onrender.com.
  2. Click on the Settings button in the top navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone section.
  4. Click the Delete my Cloudflow account button.
  5. Type DELETE in the confirmation prompt to permanently remove your data.

Method 2: Manual Request

If you cannot access your account, you can request data deletion by emailing us at support@cloudflow.com. Please include the email address associated with your account. We will process your request within 48 hours.

What data is deleted?

Upon deletion, we permanently remove the following from our database:

  • Your email and encrypted password.
  • All Instagram automation rules and keywords.
  • All stored Facebook/Instagram access tokens.
  • Your ID mapping data.

Note: This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
